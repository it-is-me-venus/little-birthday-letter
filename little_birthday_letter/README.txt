LITTLE BIRTHDAY LETTER — QUICK START

Files:
- index.html  (page content and birthday letter)
- style.css   (pink theme and mobile-friendly layout)
- script.js   (interactive envelope and replay button)
- photos/     (put your own photos here)

ADD YOUR PHOTOS
1. Put three JPG images in the photos folder.
2. Name them photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, and photo3.jpg.
3. Open index.html in a browser to preview.

PERSONALIZE THE LETTER
Open index.html in a text editor and edit the text inside the letter-card section.
The date you supplied is October 10, 2026; this version does not display a countdown, so it will still work after the birthday.

PUBLISH FOR FREE WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Sign in or create a GitHub account at https://github.com/
2. Create a new PUBLIC repository, for example: little-birthday-letter
3. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js, README.txt, and the photos folder with its contents.
4. In the repository, open Settings -> Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch.
6. Choose branch main and folder /(root), then Save.
7. Wait a minute or two. The Pages section will show your public website URL, usually:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/little-birthday-letter/
8. Open the link on your phone to test it, then send that URL to him.

IMPORTANT
- The repository and website are public, so anyone with the URL may view the page and photos.
- Do not upload private details or photos you do not have permission to share.
- No server, database, or paid domain is required for this static site.
